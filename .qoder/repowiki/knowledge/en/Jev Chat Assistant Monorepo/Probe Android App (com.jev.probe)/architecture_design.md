The package `com.jev.probe` is the Android application root with no central orchestrator or DI container; wiring happens through the Android manifest registering two Services (`ChatCaptureService`, `KeepAliveService`) and three Activities (`MainActivity`, `SettingsActivity`, `KnowledgeActivity`), plus cross-child communication via the shared `core.Prefs` contract and data classes in `core.ChatModels` / `core.kb.KbModels`. `ui_shell` owns the user-facing Activities and reads/writes `Prefs` to drive the master toggle, cloud mode, and knowledge-base editor. `capture` provides the `AccessibilityService` pipeline that consumes `core.kb.KbStore` for context and renders results through `overlay.OverlayController` as a floating window. `jev_client` exposes `JevClient`/`JudgeClient`/`ReplyClient`/`VisionClient` over `HttpURLConnection` JSON; both the capture pipeline and the billing flow in `billing/` call these clients. `billing` (`EntitlementRepo`, `PlanActivity`) is the hosted-mode entry point gated by `Prefs.cloudEnabled/cloudConsent` and invoked from `MainActivity`. Children do not import each other directly — they communicate only through `Prefs` keys and the shared model types in `core`.
